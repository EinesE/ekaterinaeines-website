Research

Research areas

My research concerns evaluation, criteria, documentation, and reconstructability in institutional processes.

SMT and S-MAF

Self-Matryoshka Theory (SMT) and the Self-Matryoshka Analytical Framework (S-MAF) form part of a developing research programme. The published record includes conceptual notes, methodological work, and source-oriented analyses.

Criteriology

This strand examines criteria and the extent to which the basis of an evaluation can be examined or reconstructed.
